Before you hire

Straight answers

Where does a new piece of work start?
By email. Say what has to be decided, what the data is, whether it can be shared, and the date you need it. The public projects on this site are the work sample. A result the data cannot support will not be promised.
Do you invent client logos or testimonials?
No. The public work is the cereals series and the analyst job-market queries. If a figure is not in those repositories, it is not on this site.
What should I send before we start?
The decision the number has to support, what the data is, whether it can be shared, and a date. A tool name is not a brief.
Which tools do you work in?
Excel and PostgreSQL now, with Python and Pandas for preparation and exploration. Power BI and Tableau are part of the reporting toolkit as it grows. Git holds the work.
