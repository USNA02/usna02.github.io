About

A practice built on files that can be checked.

BSc in Information Science. Excel, PostgreSQL, and Python in daily use. Power BI and Tableau as the reporting toolkit grows. The public work is on GitHub.

I have a BSc in Information Science and a professional interest in the intersection of data analysis, information systems, research and business intelligence.

My current toolkit includes Excel and PostgreSQL, alongside ongoing development in Python, Pandas and Power BI. I also use research, documentation and analytical communication to make findings understandable and useful.

My goal is to help businesses turn their data into clear evidence that supports practical decisions. A colleague should be able to see what changed, what was compared, and what was left out.

How I work

Five checks before a number is shared.

  1. 01

    Name the decision the number has to support.

  2. 02

    Name the grain: one row is one what?

  3. 03

    Write the query or the script.

  4. 04

    Check it against a total you already believe.

  5. 05

    Say the result in a paragraph, including what it does not prove.
